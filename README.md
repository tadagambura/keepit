# KeepIt

**Receipts, returns and warranties, never lost**

> #22 of 33 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Whenever you buy something · Harder · Revenue potential: Medium · Home & Household

## What it is

Snap a receipt and KeepIt remembers the return window and warranty expiry, reminds you before they run out, and keeps everything searchable for when something breaks.

## Why it's in the portfolio

People lose money every year to missed returns and lost receipts. Reminders bring users back, and it pairs with WorthIt: what you bought, and whether it was worth it.

## Who it's for

Anyone who buys electronics, appliances or clothes and loses receipts.

**The problem:** Receipts fade and go missing, and return windows close before people act.

## Version one

- Photo receipts with item, shop, price and date
- Return-window and warranty-expiry reminders
- Search by item or shop
- Categories
- Manual entry for online orders

**Example:** A user's headphones break at 11 months; KeepIt finds the receipt and shows a month of warranty left.

## Later

- Read receipt text automatically, on the device
- Cloud backup (premium)
- Manuals and serial numbers
